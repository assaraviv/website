About

Assa Raviv is a Tel Aviv-based composer and music producer writing original music for advertising, film and television.

He has scored campaigns for Apple, Google, Nike, BMW and Amazon, as well as Super Bowl commercials, and his work has earned Silver and Bronze Clio Awards. His sonic identities, including The Sound of Leffe, have won Gold and Bronze at the Transform Awards.

On television, his credits include Losing Alice (Apple TV+), Cold Case: The Tylenol Murders (Netflix) and the Emmy-nominated docuseries Buried (Showtime).
