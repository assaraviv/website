About

Assa Raviv is a Tel Aviv-based composer and music producer specializing in original scores for film, television, commercials, and sonic branding. With over a decade of experience, his work is defined by a seamless blend of cinematic storytelling, emotional clarity, and a meticulously refined approach to sound design.

His music has powered international campaigns for iconic brands including Apple, Google, and Nike spanning global broadcast and digital content, highlighted by multiple Super Bowl commercials and recognized with Silver and Bronze Clio Awards.

A versatile storyteller, Assa has composed original scores for leading platforms such as Netflix, Showtime, and Apple TV+. Key credits include the psychological thriller Losing Alice, the Netflix series Cold Case: The Tylenol Murders, and the Emmy-nominated Showtime documentary series Buried.

Beyond the screen, Assa partners with global brands to develop distinct audio identities and sound logos work recognized with Gold and Bronze Transform Awards.
