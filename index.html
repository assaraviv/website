About

Assa Raviv is a Tel Aviv-based composer and music producer writing original music for advertising, film and television.

He has scored campaigns for Apple, Google, Nike, BMW and Amazon, as well as Super Bowl commercials, and his work has earned Silver and Bronze Clio Awards. His sonic identities have won Gold and Bronze at the Transform Awards, and The Sound of Leffe was shortlisted at D&AD.

On television, his credits include Losing Alice (Apple TV+), Cold Case: The Tylenol Murders (Netflix) and the Emmy-nominated docuseries Buried (Showtime).
